Pet Safety Tips

🐾 Keep plants on stable stands.
💧 Avoid fertilizers containing harmful chemicals.
🌱 Always identify unknown plants before bringing them home.
🐶 Provide pet grass to reduce chewing.

Why Pet-Safe Plants?

Many common houseplants are toxic to cats and dogs. Choosing pet-safe varieties lets you decorate your home confidently while keeping your furry companions healthy and happy.
